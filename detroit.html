About Detroit

Detroit was founded in 1701 as a French trading post by French explorer Antoine de la Mothe Cadillac. The Fort Pontchartrain du Détroit later bacame a vital trading hub for Indigenous peoples, including the Anishinaabe, as well as an important center for the fur trade and a strategic outpost connecting the Great Lakes (City of Detroit, 2026).

Did you know?

The Detroit River is one of the few rivers in North America that flows south (City of Detroit, 2026). Detroit was also the orginal capital of Michigan before Lansing became the capital in 1847 (Michigan Legislature, n.d.).

Detroit was incorporated as a city in 1815 and by the mid-20th century, has bceome one of the largest cities in the United States. Detroit has also become synonymous with the automobile industry, led by innovators like Henry Ford, who revolutionized manufacturing with the moving assembly line in 1913 (City of Detroit, 2026).

City Facts

  • Median income of Detroit is $39,938 compared to Michigan's median income of $72,875 (U.S. Census Bureau, n.d.)
  • In 2025 the population was 649,095 (U.S. Census Bureau, n.d.)
  • Detroit is located in south east Michigan
  • Detroit was incorporated in 1815 (Detroit Historical Society, n.d.)
  • Detroit is a Urban City (Census Reporter, n.d.)
