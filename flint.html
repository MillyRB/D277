About Flint

Flint was orginally founded by a fur trader named Jacob Smith in 1819. During the nineteenth century the village was major lumbering area. Starting the the late 1800s to the mid-twentieth century, Flint was a leading manufacturer of carriages then automobiles, earning it the moniker “Vehicle City” (City of Flint, 2026)

Did you know?

Flint the Michigan's 12th largest city (City of Flint, 2026).

Flint is also known for being the birthplace of General Motors. Later on Flint became know as an automobile manufacturing powerhouse for due to Genral Motor's Buick and Chevrolet division. Additionally, for classic car lovers, Flint host an annual week long "Back to the Bricks" classic car show. Flint isn't all about the steel, they host many art festivals, fairs, local craft shows Professional and semi-professional sporting events and much more (City of Flint, 2026).

City Facts

  • Median income of Flint is $37,646 compared to Michigan's median income of $72,875 (U.S. Census Bureau, n.d.)
  • In 2025 the population was 79,306 (U.S. Census Bureau, n.d.)
  • Flint is located in central Michigan, just 66 miles northwest of Detroit (City of Flint, 2026)
  • Flint was incorporated in 1855 (Zippia, n.d.)
  • Flint is a Urban City (Census Reporter, n.d.)
